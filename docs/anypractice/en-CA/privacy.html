Privacy policy

Your material. Your choice.

Local library

Your extracted study text, lessons, cards and progress are saved in this app on your device. The app does not create an AnyPractice account or provide its own cloud sync. Device backups may include local app data.

Photos and PDFs

Text recognition runs on your device. Imported photos and PDFs are also saved locally with your material. For Pro lessons, you can send the photo or complete selected PDF pages to OpenAI after reviewing and confirming them. Text-only modes send only reviewed text.

AI providers

Without active Pro, generation requires your own API key. Pro uses our service by default unless you explicitly choose another provider. Apple Intelligence is a Pro-only option for source-based flashcard drafts when your device and both source and question languages are supported. Generating lessons, summaries and quizzes requires a cloud connection. Expired Pro blocks Pro generation; unavailable Apple blocks the selected Apple route. The app does not switch providers automatically. Use a personal API key with OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini or a compatible provider. Your provider bills API usage separately. With your API key, only the selected passages and generation instructions are sent directly to the provider and model you confirm. Your key is stored in this device’s Keychain. The provider may retain requests under its own terms. AnyPractice cannot delete copies already held by that provider.

Apple Intelligence

An active Pro subscription is required for this option. Apple Intelligence creates source-based flashcards on supported devices. Generation and supported translation run locally; language assets may need a download.

AnyPractice Pro

When using the Pro cloud service: With Pro, selected text and any confirmed original pages pass through AnyPractice on Vercel to OpenAI. Original files are processed for that request without a server file library. Apple processes payments; the server verifies your subscription and stores identifiers and usage counters. Provider and hosting services handle data under their own policies.

Remove saved data

Delete a deck to remove its saved text, local photo or PDF copy, generated material and progress from this app. Other decks using the same material keep their copies. Remove connection deletes the saved personal key. Files outside this app, backups and provider-side data are separate.

Service records and provider retention

When using the Pro cloud service: Pro passes confirmed material through Vercel to OpenAI. We do not maintain a server study-file library or intentionally log study content. This is not zero retention: provider and infrastructure policies still apply. OpenAI requests use store:false; default abuse-monitoring content may remain for up to 30 days with legal/safety exceptions. Subscription access, security and limits use signed Apple transactions, stable hashed subscription/request identifiers and UTC counters. These identifiers are not anonymous. Daily counters expire 3 days after their last update, monthly counters after 35 days; duplicate-request digests do not automatically expire. Deleting a local deck does not delete these records, provider records, OS backups or support correspondence. Contact us about service data requests; never send a secret key or password.

Support

No learner account, advertising SDKs or cross-app tracking. This website uses no analytics cookies or advertising scripts. GitHub Pages still processes ordinary hosting/network requests. Support email and information you choose to send are used to respond and investigate issues. Apple handles payments; we do not receive card details or Apple passwords. AnyPractice is intended for adults, parents and teachers.
