Privacy policy

Your material. Your choice.

Local library and recognition

No AnyPractice learner account is required. Decks, lessons, edits, study progress and retained original photos/PDFs are stored in the app on your device. Text recognition runs on the device. Device or cloud backups controlled by your operating system may include app data. With an active Pro subscription, you can explicitly choose Apple Intelligence for source-based flashcard drafts when your device and both source and question languages are supported. Generation runs locally. Generating lessons, summaries and quizzes requires a cloud connection.

What goes to an AI provider

Without active Pro, generation requires your own API key. Pro uses our service by default unless you explicitly choose another provider. Apple Intelligence is a Pro-only option for source-based flashcard drafts when your device and both source and question languages are supported. Generating lessons, summaries and quizzes requires a cloud connection. Expired Pro blocks Pro generation; unavailable Apple blocks the selected Apple route. The app does not switch providers automatically. Use a personal API key with OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini or a compatible provider. Your provider bills API usage separately.

Cloud generation sends the material you review and confirm. Text may include document titles, selected passages, page references and the language and learning options needed for the request. Treat private, copyrighted or sensitive material carefully and send only material you have the right to use.

Pro processing and retention

Our API does not use a study-material database or intentionally write study material into application logs. This does not mean zero retention: infrastructure providers and OpenAI process the request under their own policies. OpenAI API requests use store: false. OpenAI's default abuse-monitoring logs can retain content for up to 30 days, with stated legal and safety exceptions; other provider data controls may apply. We have not promised a Zero Data Retention arrangement. See OpenAI API data controls.

For subscription access, security, usage limits and duplicate-request prevention, the service processes signed Apple transaction information, product/environment information, a stable hashed subscription identifier, hashed request identifiers and UTC usage counters. Hashing does not make a stable identifier anonymous. Daily counters expire 3 days after their last update and monthly counters 35 days after their last update; the usable quota resets by UTC date/calendar month. Duplicate-request digests do not automatically expire. Service and infrastructure logs may contain operational request/error and network information. Do not assume these records disappear when a deck is deleted.

Purchases and support

Apple handles payments. We do not receive your payment-card details or Apple password. Purchase verification is used to provide the subscription and enforce its limits. If you contact support or report content, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send, and use it to respond, investigate and maintain the service. We do not require private study documents or API secrets for support.

Advertising and tracking

The app does not include advertising or cross-app tracking SDKs, and we do not use your study material for advertising. This site uses no analytics cookies or third-party advertising scripts; GitHub Pages still processes ordinary hosting/network requests under its own policies.

Deletion and your choices

Deleting a deck removes that local deck, its retained originals and study progress. Other deck copies, backups, support correspondence and provider-side records are separate. Removing an API connection removes its saved key from the app's Keychain storage. Cancelling a subscription does not itself erase data. We cannot remotely erase your on-device library. To ask about access, correction or deletion of information handled by our service, email us; we may need enough information to verify the request without asking for a secret key or password. Provider retention, legal requirements and security records can limit deletion.

Audience and changes

AnyPractice is intended for adults, parents and teachers. It is not a children's account or public social service. AI outputs can be inaccurate; review them before use. We may update this policy when the service changes and will change the date above.
