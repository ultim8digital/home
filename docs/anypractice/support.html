Photos and PDFs

Report generated content

AI can make mistakes. You can edit or delete generated material in the app. To report harmful content, describe the issue without including private information.

Remove saved data

Delete a deck to remove its saved text, local photo or PDF copy, generated material and progress from this app. Other decks using the same material keep their copies. Remove connection deletes the saved personal key. Files outside this app, backups and provider-side data are separate.
